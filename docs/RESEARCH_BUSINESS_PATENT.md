# Research, patent, and business brief

## Research paper structure
1. Abstract and autonomous-logistics failure context
2. Related work: centralized dispatch, swarm robotics, edge coordination
3. Decentralized architecture and utility auction
4. Communication-aware self-healing protocol
5. Experimental design: 5/25/50/100 agents and seven failure scenarios
6. Results: success, recovery time, collision rate, overhead, delivery time
7. Threats to validity and future hardware validation

## Patent potential

Potentially protectable novelty should be validated by a patent professional: a communication-risk-weighted local auction that reassigns orphaned logistics tasks using neighbor state freshness, battery reserve, and collision risk; plus a recovery lease that prevents duplicate deliveries during mesh partitions.

## Startup model

Sell the coordination layer as a fleet resilience SDK to warehouse robotics, drone logistics, and industrial inspection operators. Initial wedge: a simulation-backed resilience audit; expansion: edge runtime licensing, scenario testing, and fleet observability subscriptions. Key proof point is reduced recovery time without a central controller.
